Kralın Sözü

How it plays

No bars or numbers on screen. You read everything from the world.

  1. Question

    Ask for a name, where they came from and why. Check the seal, the wax colour, the moon stamp and the approving clerk against the order board.

  2. Open or turn away

    Turning away an honest courier costs as much as letting a traitor in.

  3. Keep warm

    You feel the cold in your breath, the frost creeping in at the edges and your heartbeat. Wood is scarce. Traitors offer wine and furs; take them and the men on the walls will see.

  4. Listen to the dark

    Howling from the forest means wolves; torches on the frozen river mean bandits. Face the right way and sound the horn, and the archers loose their arrows.

  5. Keep their trust

    If the guards in the towers stop trusting you, they turn their crossbows on you.

Making of

A one-person project: code, balance and performance.

  1. A scene built in code

    The whole scene is built in code. The rules live in a core that doesn't depend on Unity.

  2. 2,000 nights of balance

    I tuned the difficulty with a 2,000-night simulation and built automated test bots that run without opening a window.

  3. No clock

    The moon tells the time: it rises behind the mountains on the right and is overhead at midnight. A castle bell rings on the hour.

  4. Made to adjust

    Three difficulty levels, key remapping, controller support and subtitle size. Instead of voice acting, every character has its own speech blip.

  5. Fast

    Built with Vulkan. Average frame time on an RTX 4060 at 1080p, High: 1.6 ms.

Team game, lead programmer · Since Jul 2026

Under the Counter

A dark-humour first-person shop sim: you run a small 2000s shop selling bootleg CDs. Target: a Steam Next Fest demo.

I write the game's systems: the economy, impatient customers, police raids driven by a suspicion meter and shopping from an in-game computer.

I built a crash-safe save system and TR/EN localization. Most of the team's commits are mine.

The game has 88 runtime scripts and 28 editor tools that speed up the team's work. The goal is a demo at Steam Next Fest.

  • 88 Runtime scripts
  • 28 Editor tools

Team of 3

Unity 6, URP, NavMesh, Input System, C#

YAZITAŞ · Issue 2 · Games9

Mobile game · 2026

Pati Rotası

A mobile game that gets you walking. Photograph street cats to build a collection, clear the fog on the map as you walk, and turn your steps into food points for animal shelters.

32 cat breeds, rarity levels and neighbourhood albums.

I added a step counter, streaks, daily quests and badges.

I wanted to see whether game mechanics can nudge people toward a real habit like getting outside. The Android APK is on GitHub.

Expo, React Native, Leaflet
